# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Be welcoming to people regardless of experience, background, identity or
  where they are from.
- Give feedback on the work. "This breaks when the end date is empty" helps;
  "this is amateur" does not.
- Accept feedback the same way. Being wrong about something is normal.
- Remember that most people here are doing this in their spare time.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Raise anything that concerns you with the maintainer, privately, at
**eamenuveve05@gmail.com**. Reports are handled in confidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
do not follow this document, and may ban anyone whose behaviour warrants it.
Repeated or severe breaches will not get repeated warnings.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened for a project of this size.
